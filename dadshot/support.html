常见问题

点了「生成高光」，提示没找到照片？

DadShot 只从你授权的照片里挑选。请检查：① 系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → DadShot」是否给了访问权限；② 首页顶部的取片范围（本周 / 本月 / 最近添加）里是否确实有照片；③ 若设置了「宝宝识别」，则只会挑选含该宝宝的照片，可在设置里调整匹配松紧或清除参考脸。

怎么让它只挑我家宝宝？

设置 → 宝宝识别 → 选几张清晰的宝宝正脸作参考。之后生成高光时只会挑含该宝宝的照片。人脸特征只保存在本机，可随时清除。

「保存」存出来的是会动的照片？

是的。设置 → 保存类型里可以分别控制高光、单图、手账存为实况照片（Live Photo）还是普通静图。存为实况时，整幅拼图会有轻微运镜；若格子里的照片本身就是实况照片，它们还会各自播放自己的动效。

我的照片会被上传吗？

不会。DadShot 没有服务器、没有账号，所有处理都在你的设备本地完成。唯一的联网是：当照片自带 GPS 坐标时，通过 Apple 的系统服务把坐标换成地名——只发送坐标，不涉及照片内容。详见隐私政策。

购买后水印还在 / 换了手机怎么恢复？

解锁为一次性买断。若购买后水印仍在，请重新打开 App；换新机或重装后，到设置 → 解锁 → 「恢复购买」即可找回，需登录当初购买时使用的 Apple 账户。

删除 App 后数据还在吗？

不在。所有记录（高光、宝宝档案、设置）只存在设备本地的应用沙盒里，删除 App 即全部删除，且无法找回。已保存到系统相册的成片不受影响。

FAQ

"Create Highlight" says no photos were found

DadShot only reads photos you've allowed it to access. Check that: (1) Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → DadShot has access; (2) the selected range on the home screen (This Week / This Month / Recent) actually contains photos; (3) if Baby Recognition is enabled, only photos of that baby are selected — you can loosen the match threshold or clear the reference faces in Settings.

How do I make it pick only my baby?

Settings → Baby Recognition → add a few clear front-facing photos as references. Face features are stored only on your device and can be cleared at any time.

What is "Save as Live Photo"?

In Settings → Save Type you can choose, separately for highlights, single photos and journals, whether to save as a Live Photo or a still image. When saved as a Live Photo, the whole collage gets a gentle pan-and-zoom; any cells that are themselves Live Photos also play their own motion.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. DadShot has no server and no account — everything runs locally on your device. The only network call is reverse geocoding: when a photo carries GPS coordinates, Apple's system service turns them into a place name. Only coordinates are sent, never photo content. See the Privacy Policy.

I purchased the unlock but still see the watermark / how do I restore it?

The unlock is a one-time purchase. If the watermark persists, relaunch the app. On a new device or after reinstalling, go to Settings → Unlock → "Restore Purchase", signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Is my data kept after deleting the app?

No. All records (highlights, baby profile, settings) live only in the app's sandbox on your device and are removed with the app — this cannot be undone. Images already saved to your photo library are unaffected.
