TripStamp 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 28 日

TripStamp 是一款本地优先的旅行照片卡片工具。App 没有账号体系，不使用开发者运营的服务器，也不含广告或行为分析 SDK。

照片与元数据

你选择的照片在设备本地处理，用于生成卡片、提取配色，并读取可选元数据（如拍摄日期与位置）。照片本身不会上传到 TripStamp 或开发者运营的服务器。保存和分享只在你主动选择后发生。

地点标签

当所选照片包含 GPS 元数据时，TripStamp 可能将其坐标发送给 Apple 的地理编码服务以生成地点标签。用途仅限于生成该标签，你可以在导出前修改或删除它。TripStamp 从不申请持续定位权限。

旅行足迹

当你成功保存或分享一张卡片后，TripStamp 可以把这次记忆记录到仅保存在本设备的旅行档案中。档案会保存：

  • 所选照片的元数据：拍摄日期、坐标、地点名称
  • 一张压缩后的小尺寸预览图

原始照片不会被复制进档案，也不会上传。你可以随时在设置中关闭「记录旅行足迹」，并分别删除全部足迹或全部作品——删除足迹不会影响已生成的卡片，删除作品也不会影响足迹统计。

App 内购买

TripStamp Pro 为一次性买断，通过 Apple 的 App 内购买系统销售。Apple 处理支付信息；TripStamp 不接触也不存储你的支付卡数据。

数据存储与删除

导出偏好、免费导出计数与旅行档案均仅保存在设备本地。你可以从 Apple 照片中删除已导出的卡片、在设置中删除足迹与作品，并通过删除 App 清除其全部本地数据。

TripStamp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

TripStamp is a local-first travel photo card tool. It has no account system, uses no developer-operated servers, and contains no advertising or behavioural analytics SDKs.

Photos and metadata

Photos you choose are processed on your device to create cards, extract colours, and read optional metadata such as date and location. The photo itself is not uploaded to TripStamp or a developer-operated server. Saving and sharing happen only after you choose those actions.

Location labels

When a selected photo contains GPS metadata, TripStamp may send its coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to generate a place label. This is limited to that label, and you can edit or remove it before export. TripStamp never requests ongoing location access.

Travel footprints

When you successfully save or share a card, TripStamp can record that memory in a travel archive stored only on this device. The archive keeps:

  • metadata from the photo you chose: capture date, coordinates, place names
  • a small compressed preview image

Original photos are never copied into the archive and nothing is uploaded. You can turn off "Record travel footprints" at any time in Settings, and delete all footprints or all creations separately — deleting footprints keeps your saved cards, and deleting creations keeps your footprint statistics.

In-app purchases

TripStamp Pro is a one-time purchase sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment information; TripStamp does not receive or store payment card data.

Storage and deletion

Export preferences, the free export counter and the travel archive stay on your device. Remove exported cards from Apple Photos, delete footprints and creations in Settings, and delete the app to remove all of its local data.
