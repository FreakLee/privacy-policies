Speaker Cleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

We collect nothing

Speaker Cleaner runs entirely on your device. This app:

  • Does not collect any personal data
  • Does not use the microphone (it only plays sound through the speaker)
  • Contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you
  • Works fully offline — no network connection required

In-app purchase

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment information.
